# Privacy Policy — Vapor Apocalypse

**Effective date: 13 August 2026**

Vapor Apocalypse ("the Game") is an Android game developed by VaporRainbow Labs ("we", "us").
This policy explains what data the Game collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can
have it deleted.

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone
number, contacts, photos, files, or location.

## What we collect

The Game uses **GameAnalytics** (GameAnalytics ApS) to understand how the Game performs and how
it is played. Through GameAnalytics, the following is collected automatically when you play:

- **An App Set ID.** This is an identifier provided by Google Play services. It is not your name
  and it is not your device's advertising identifier. It is scoped to us as a developer and is
  reset when you uninstall all of our apps from the device.
- **Gameplay events.** For example, that a run started. These events carry no personal
  information — the Game is designed so that only a small, fixed set of non-identifying fields
  can be attached to an event.
- **Technical and performance information.** Frame timing measurements, and error or crash
  reports, used to find bugs and performance problems.
- **Basic device and app information** collected by GameAnalytics as part of the above, such as
  device model, operating system version, country, and app version.

The Game does **not** contain advertisements, does not use the Android advertising identifier
(AAID), and does not request the `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` permission.

## Why we collect it

Only to operate and improve the Game: to see whether it runs smoothly on real devices, to find
crashes, and to understand which parts of the Game are played. We do not use this data to build
advertising profiles and we do not sell it.

## Who it is shared with

- **GameAnalytics ApS**, as our analytics provider. Their privacy policy:
  <https://gameanalytics.com/privacy>

We do not share your data with anyone else.

## Legal basis (EU / UK users)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on your **consent** for analytics. You can withdraw
your consent at any time by contacting us at the address below, or by uninstalling the Game.

## Data retention

Analytics data is retained by GameAnalytics according to their retention policy. We do not keep a
separate copy.

## How to have your data deleted

Email **vaporrainbow.support@gmail.com** with the subject line **"Data deletion request"**.

Please include your device model and roughly when you played, so that we can locate the records.
We will action the request and confirm by email **within 30 days**.

You may also uninstall the Game at any time, which stops all further collection.

## Children

The Game is **not directed to children**. It is rated for ages 13 and over, and we do not
knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has played the Game
and you would like their data removed, email **vaporrainbow.support@gmail.com** and we will delete it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the
processing of your data, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these, email
**vaporrainbow.support@gmail.com**.

## Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect — for example when advertisements or in-app purchases are added — we
will update this page and change the effective date above.

## Contact

VaporRainbow Labs — **vaporrainbow.support@gmail.com**
